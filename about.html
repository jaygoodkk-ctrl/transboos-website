About

TRANSBOOS

Precision Braking. Zero Compromise.

TRANSBOOS was founded by brake engineers who were tired of road-caliber parts failing on track. We build one thing, and only one thing, to an uncompromising standard: the ability to slow a fast car down, repeatedly, without drama. From a single workshop to a global distribution network, every TRANSBOOS kit is designed, tested and validated by people who actually race.
