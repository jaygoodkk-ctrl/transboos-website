Technology

Engineering the stop, not just the part

TRANSBOOS systems are designed around one constraint: heat. Everything below exists to manage it.

01

Forged Monoblock

A single forged billet removes the flex and leak paths of multi-piece calipers, so clamp force goes to the pad — not into the casting.

02

Thermal Control

Curved internal vanes pump air through the disc, while floating bells let the ring expand freely and resist warping under heat.

03

Track Validation

Every compound and caliper is dyno-mapped then proven in endurance racing before it earns a part number.

04

Precision Manufacturing

Five-axis CNC, CMM inspection and batch traceability on every component we ship.

Proven where it counts
